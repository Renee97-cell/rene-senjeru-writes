Environment · Regional Cooperation

Can ACTO Save the Amazon? Regional Cooperation at the Tipping Point

The Amazon has lost over 17% of its original forest cover. Eight countries share it, yet no single policy framework has been able to stop deforestation. This paper uses liberal institutionalism and constructivism to assess why the Amazon Cooperation Treaty Organization succeeds in norm-building but fails in enforcement — and what the 2023 Belém Declaration really means for the forest's future.
